2. Typography Hierarchy

Poppins (black/bold) for headings, Inter for everything else. Fixed scale only — no arbitrary sizes.

H1 — Find your perfect trainer

Poppins · font-black (900) · text-3xl lg:text-4xl

H2 — Section heading

Poppins · font-black (900) · text-2xl lg:text-3xl

H3 — Card heading

Poppins · font-bold (700) · text-xl

H4 — Small heading

Poppins · font-bold (700) · text-lg

Body text — Certified trainers, verified reviews, and instant WhatsApp contact. This is the default reading size across Onlifit.
